# Exclusive breastfeeding, at least 6 months

SOURCE: Statistics Canada, table 13-10-0905-01 (Canadian Community Health Survey).
NOTE IDS: 31, 33, 34
URL: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310090501
RETRIEVED: 2026-07-31
CATEGORY: indicator definition
TABLE MEMBERS: Exclusive breastfeeding, at least 6 months

## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 31.** Based on information provided by females aged 18 to 55 who had a baby in the last 5 years.

**Note 33.** Exclusive breastfeeding refers to an infant receiving only breast milk, without any additional liquid (even water) or solid food.

**Note 34.** The numerator includes mothers who have exclusively breastfed for at least 6 months and who may or may not be still breastfeeding. The denominator includes all mothers who had a baby in the past 5 years but excludes mothers who were still breastfeeding and who had not introduced any other liquids or solid foods to the baby's feeds.
