# Quality flags E and F

CATEGORY: data quality

## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 44.** Quality flags applied to percentage estimates are based on the sample size within the domain of estimation and based on the coefficient of variation (CV) for the estimated number of persons. The flag (E) signifies an estimate with poorer quality which should be used with caution. The flag (F) signifies an estimate with a quality that is too unreliable to be published. When there is no quality flag applied to the data, users should use the estimated confidence intervals as an indicator of quality for the statistics.
