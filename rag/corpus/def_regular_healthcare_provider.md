## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 38.** Measures the proportion individuals aged 18 and over who reported having a healthcare provider they regularly see or talk to when they need normal care or advice for their health. It includes one of the following healthcare providers: family doctor or general practitioner, medical specialist (for example, cardiologist, pediatrician, oncologist), nurse practitioner or other (for example, chiropractor, psychologist).
