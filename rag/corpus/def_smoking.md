# Current smoker

SOURCE: Statistics Canada, table 13-10-0905-01 (Canadian Community Health Survey).
NOTE IDS: 21, 22, 23, 25
URL: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1310090501
RETRIEVED: 2026-07-31
CATEGORY: indicator definition
TABLE MEMBERS: Current smoker, daily; Current smoker, daily or occasional

## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 21.** Population aged 18 and over who reported being a current smoker. Does not take into account the number of cigarettes smoked.

**Note 22.** Data collected for this indicator is based on the question referring to smoking of cigarettes only. Note that data on smoking alternative tobacco products is captured in a different module (TAL).

**Note 23.** Daily smoker refers to those who reported smoking cigarettes every day.

**Note 25.** Occasional smoker refers to those who reported smoking cigarettes occasionally. This includes former daily smokers who now smoke occasionally.
