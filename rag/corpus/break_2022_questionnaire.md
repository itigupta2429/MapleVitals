## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 49.** The 2022 cycle of the Canadian Community Health Survey (CCHS) introduced a redesigned questionnaire and a new collection method, with most responses collected through online self-reporting and the remainder by interviewer (about 40% of the completed cases). Users must carefully consider the potential impact of methodological changes on observed differences and use caution when comparing 2022 data with previous cycles.
