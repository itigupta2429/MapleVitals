## StatCan text (verbatim)

**Note 31.** Based on information provided by females aged 18 to 55 who had a baby in the last 5 years.

**Note 32.** Initiated breastfeeding refers to mothers who breastfed or tried to breastfeed their last child, or gave breastmilk to their last child even if only for a short time.
